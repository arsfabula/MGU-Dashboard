# MGU Dashboard — Quick Start Guide

An **unofficial** Connect IQ data field for e-bikes (Pinion MGU motor): arc-shaped power gauge, Motor / Cadence / Assist band, Battery / Range band, bike name at the bottom. Connects automatically.

> Note: this data field uses the Sigma protocol to connect to your eBike. To enable it, the eBike must have been connected at least once to the FIT Maintenance Tool at a specialist shop or to the FIT E-Bike Control app.

## Install

- **Phone (recommended)**: Garmin Connect ▸ **Connect IQ Store** ▸ **MGU Dashboard** ▸ **Install** ▸ pick your watch.
- **Computer**: connect.garmin.com ▸ Connect IQ Store ▸ Install.

## Add to an activity

- **On the watch**: activity ▸ middle button ▸ **Data Screens** ▸ **Add** ▸ **Connect IQ** ▸ MGU Dashboard.
- **Phone**: Garmin Connect ▸ **More** ▸ activity ▸ **Data Screens** ▸ **Add** ▸ Connect IQ ▸ MGU Dashboard.

## Settings

Activity ▸ **Activity Settings** ▸ **Connect IQ Fields** ▸ **MGU Dashboard**.

- **Demo mode**: simulated data, no bike.
- **Labels**: each metric's name (on by default).
- **Metrics**: Motor, Cadence, Assist, Battery, Range — show or hide. The gauge is always shown.
- **FTP**: **Auto** by default, your Garmin profile FTP (else 200 W). The gauge scale is **2× FTP**; otherwise a manual value from 60 to 400 W.

## Recording

Continuous (power, cadence, assist, battery, range), plus summary and per lap, in Garmin Connect.

## Troubleshooting

- "Scanning…" all the time: move closer, motor on.
- "Waiting…": restart the activity or power-cycle the motor.
- No pairing: bike already paired, or forget it in the watch's Bluetooth settings.
- Needle pinned at the top: "Auto" FTP too low, pick a value.
- No bike, or white screen in the simulator: turn **Demo mode** on, then off.
